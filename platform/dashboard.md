# Dashboard

Your home page after signing in — a snapshot of your compute balance, recent activity, and a quick way to try a circuit.

## Compute tokens

At the top of the dashboard, the **Compute tokens** card shows your remaining balance and a link to purchase more. If your balance is low, a banner appears here as a reminder.

## Latest jobs

A short list of your most recently submitted jobs, each with its live status. Select **View all jobs** to go to the full [Jobs](/platform/jobs) page, or click a row here to inspect its measurement results.

## Getting started tiles

Two quick-start tiles let you try the platform without leaving the dashboard:

- **Submit sample circuits** — runs a preset circuit (H gate or Bell state) against a free simulator and shows the measurement results inline. This never costs compute tokens.
- **Get Entropy** — generates certified randomness from Light Rider's entropy service (EMS) on demand. See the [Entropy Management System docs](/entropy/introduction) for how the underlying service works.

## Before you sign in

If you aren't signed in, the dashboard shows a welcome view instead, with links to browse [Backends](/platform/backends) and the [SDK documentation](/sdk/getting-started), plus a **Create an account** button.
